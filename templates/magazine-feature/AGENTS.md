# Magazine feature

Use this template as an adaptable starting point for the feature, with room for variation and creativity. Its character comes from the contrast between a broad opening and an offset reading column. The specimen's measurements are defaults: a long headline may need a smaller size, different spacing, or a rebalanced opening. Adjust the column, image proportions, and placement when the material calls for it. Aim for a coherent feature, not an exact reproduction of the specimen.

Give the headline presence and usually keep the kicker and byline quiet. An opening image can span the full content width, with its caption immediately below. Flush body paragraphs and open spacing are a useful baseline. Display quotes work as occasional moments of emphasis in the reading flow. Omit or adapt these elements to suit the story; none imposes a mandatory content outline.

Accept ordinary OpenDoc blocks in the author's order. The theme supplies visual hierarchy, rhythm, and component treatments; the layout supplies adaptable dimensions and spacing. Use the shared Neutral theme for the catalog specimen. References normally follow the body. Preserve stable block IDs, and keep document-specific variations local so other documents retain their existing layout.

Use Forme's native Page, Fixed, View, and text flow through the existing OpenDoc primitives. Do not introduce the Prose indentation helper, column-balancing logic, text measurement, custom line splitting, negative margins, absolute-positioned body text, or renderer patches. The reading column is one flowing column; it does not promise newspaper-style two-column pagination. Leave variable text breakable. An image and its caption may use the existing Figure block; size the complete figure to fit a page.

Review the adapted document and its page continuations. Shared layout changes also need sparse, typical, and long specimens, a second theme, and a working starter. Do not change unrelated templates or documents.
